Salud Familiar is an intuitive, web-based health management portal designed to help families log, monitor, and manage daily health routines, medication schedules, somatic treatments, injection rotation zones, vital measurements, and sleep tracking.
---
✨ Features
💉 Injection Zone & Body Map Tracker
Interactive body zone selection (Abdomen, Thighs, Glutes).
Rotational wheel schedule to ensure proper site rotation and minimize skin irritation.
Quick logging form for dosages, times, and local reactions.
🧪 Somatropina & Specialized Medication Logs
Specialized tracking for daily growth hormone (Somatropin) and prescription medications.
Visual status badges for upcoming, completed, or resting schedules.
📊 Vitals & Health Metrics
Track weight, blood pressure, temperature, and other vital health metrics.
Simple interfaces for daily measurements and historical trends.
😴 Sleep & Wellness Logs
Monitor sleep duration, quality, and nightly routines.
📅 Interactive Calendar & History
Real-time date navigator and log history to review previous entries easily.
---
🛠️ Built With
HTML5 & CSS3 — Responsive UI design and clean layout.
JavaScript (ES6+) — Interactive body map, data persistence, and rotational algorithms.
Tailwind CSS / Custom CSS — Modern styling for accessibility and mobile responsiveness.
